NLP Researcher · Member of Technical Staff at Fastino Labs

Urchade Zaratiana

I work on small language models and structured prediction, with a particular focus on information extraction. My research explores how compact encoder models can produce reliable structured outputs for entity recognition, relation extraction, classification, safety, and privacy.

At Fastino Labs, I lead the Data Structuring team and develop the GLiNER family of models. I completed my PhD at the Laboratoire Informatique de Paris Nord (LIPN), supervised by Nadi Tomeh and Thierry Charnois.
